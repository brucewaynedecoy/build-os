# Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I paint my walls?

Yes. Use a light color, and return the walls to white when you move out.

### Who fixes a broken appliance?

The cooperative fixes appliances that came with the home. Report the fault through the maintenance tracker.

### Can I sublet my home?

No. Subletting is not allowed under the lease.

# Getting Started

Welcome to the cooperative. This guide covers your first two weeks.

## Your First Day

1. Collect your keys and fob from the front desk.
1. Read the house rules in your welcome pack.
1. Test the smoke alarm in your home.

## Your First Two Weeks

Register any pets, set up your monthly charge payment, and come to a welcome evening. The membership committee will contact you to arrange a short visit.

# Paying Monthly Charges

Monthly charges cover the cost of running the buildings. They are due on the first day of each month.

| Home size | Monthly charge | Repairs reserve share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Studio | €610 | €45 |
| One bedroom | €780 | €60 |
| Two bedrooms | €945 | €75 |

## Late Payments

If you cannot pay on time, tell the treasurer before the due date. The cooperative can agree a payment plan.
