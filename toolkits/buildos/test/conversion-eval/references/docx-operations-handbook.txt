# Sample Housing Cooperative Operations Handbook

This handbook explains how the sample housing cooperative runs day-to-day operations. It is written for new committee members and staff. Every name in it is invented.

Version 3.2 — reviewed in October 2026.
@@ section: Roles and Responsibilities @@
# 1. Roles and Responsibilities

The cooperative has three standing committees. Each committee meets once a month and reports to the board.
@@ section: Board @@
## Board

The board sets the annual budget, approves contracts over the spending limit, and hires the operations manager.
@@ section: Maintenance Committee @@
## Maintenance Committee

The maintenance committee plans repairs, reviews requests, and keeps the contractor list current.
@@ section: Membership Committee @@
## Membership Committee

The membership committee runs the waiting list, welcomes new members, and handles move-outs.
@@ section: Handling a Repair Request @@
# 2. Handling a Repair Request

Use these steps for every request, even small ones:

1. Log the request in the maintenance tracker within one working day.
1. Assign a priority:
  1. High: no heat, no water, a safety risk, or a door that will not lock.
  1. Medium: a fault that affects daily use.
  1. Low: anything else.
1. Book a contractor or a volunteer.
1. Confirm the fix with the member and close the request.
@@ section: Emergency Contacts @@
## Emergency Contacts

Out of hours: call the on-call line listed on the notice board.

- Water or gas leak: shut the valve first, then call.
- Lift fault: call the lift contractor.
- Fire: leave the building and call emergency services.
@@ section: Spending Limits @@
# 3. Spending Limits

The operations manager may approve spending up to the limits below. Anything above a limit goes to the board.

| Category | Single item limit | Monthly limit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Routine repairs | €500 | €3,000 | Two quotes over €250 |
| Cleaning supplies | €150 | €600 | Use the approved supplier |
| Emergency work | €2,000 | No limit | Report to the board within 48 hours |
| Office costs | €100 | €400 | Keep all receipts |

Limits are reviewed each January. The next review is in January 2027.
@@ section: Records @@
# 4. Records

Keep signed contracts for seven years. Keep repair photos for two years. Store both in the shared drive under the year and the building name.
