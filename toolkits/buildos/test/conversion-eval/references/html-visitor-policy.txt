# Visitor and Guest Policy

This policy explains how members host visitors and overnight guests. It applies to all three buildings.
@@ section: Day Visitors @@
## Day Visitors

Members may host day visitors at any time. Visitors must leave the common room by 22:00.
@@ section: Overnight Guests @@
## Overnight Guests

A member may host an overnight guest for up to 14 nights in any 90-day period. Longer stays need written approval from the [membership committee](/committees/membership.html).

- Tell the front desk the guest’s name and dates.
- Guests may not use the laundry room alone.
- Parking: guests use the visitor bays only.
  - Bays V1 to V4 are in the north lot.
  - Bay V5 is kept for deliveries.
@@ section: Approval Steps @@
### Approval Steps

1. Fill in the guest form.
1. Send it to the membership committee.
1. Wait for the written reply, usually within five working days.
@@ section: Callout note @@
Note: Guests who stay longer than 30 nights in a year count as occupants under the lease.
@@ section: Shared Spaces @@
## Shared Spaces

| Space | Guests allowed | Booking |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common room | Up to 10 | Book at the front desk |
| Roof garden | Up to 6 | No booking |
| Guest suite | 2 per night | Book online, €25 per night |

Route for guests: front desk, then the common room, then the guest suite.
