@@ section: Pet Policy @@
Pet Policy
This policy sets out which pets members may keep and how to keep them without disturbing
neighbours. It applies to every home and to all shared spaces.
@@ section: Pets Allowed @@
Pets Allowed
Members may keep up to two cats or one dog per home. Small caged animals and fish do not
need approval. Any other animal needs written approval from the board.

@@ section: Registration @@
Registration
Register each cat or dog with the front desk within two weeks of moving in or getting the pet.
Bring proof of vaccination. The front desk keeps the register and shares it only with the
maintenance team when they need to enter a home.
@@ section: Shared Spaces @@
Shared Spaces
Dogs must be on a lead in all corridors, lifts, and the courtyard. Pets are not allowed in the
common room, the laundry room, or the roof garden, except for assistance animals.

Complaints
If a pet causes repeated noise or damage, the membership committee will first talk with the
member. If the problem continues, the committee may ask the board to withdraw approval.
@@ section: Review @@
Review
The board will review this policy every two years.
