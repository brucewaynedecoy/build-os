Annual Report Summary 2025–26
Sample Housing Cooperative · Prepared for the annual general meeting
@@ section: Overview @@
Overview
The cooperative ended the year with all 64 homes occupied and a small operating surplus.
Members voted to keep monthly charges flat for a second year. The board thanks the
volunteers who gave more than 1,900 hours of their time.
@@ section: Membership @@
Membership
Nine households moved in and seven moved out. The waiting list grew to 112 households, the
longest it has been since the cooperative opened. The membership committee ran four
welcome evenings and one open day.
Two new members joined the board in March. Training for new board members now runs over
three evenings instead of one. Year-round support comes from a mentor on the board.
@@ section: Finances @@
Finances
Income from monthly charges was €612,400. Spending was €598,150, so the surplus was
€14,250. Most of the surplus goes to the repairs reserve, which now holds €186,000.
Energy costs fell by 9% after the boiler upgrade in the south building. Insurance rose by 6%.
@@ section: Looking Ahead @@
Looking Ahead
Next year the cooperative will replace the north building’s roof, add bicycle storage, and review
the visitor policy. The board will report on progress at each quarterly members’ meeting.
