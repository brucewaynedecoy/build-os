Roof Works Start in May
Contractors will put up scaffolding on the north
building in the first week of May. Work should take
ten weeks. Balconies on the top floor will be closed
while the scaffold is up. The maintenance committee
will post a weekly update in the lobby.
@@ section: Bike Store Plans @@
Bike Store Plans
The bike store will go in the old drying room. It will
hold 40 bikes on two-tier racks, plus space for two
cargo bikes. Members can comment on the plan until
15 December.
@@ section: New Members Welcome Evening @@
New Members Welcome Evening
Seven households joined the cooperative this
autumn. A welcome evening takes place on 28
November in the common room. Bring a dish to share
if you can.
@@ section: Garden Volunteers Needed @@
Garden Volunteers Needed
The roof garden team needs three more volunteers
for the winter. Tasks include covering the raised
beds, clearing leaves, and checking the water butts
once a week.
