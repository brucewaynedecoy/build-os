Visitor and Guest Policy

This policy explains how members host visitors and overnight guests. It applies to all three buildings.

Day Visitors

Members may host day visitors at any time. Visitors must leave the common room by 22:00.

Overnight Guests

A member may host an overnight guest for up to 14 nights in any 90-day period. Longer stays need written approval from the membership committee.

  • Tell the front desk the guest’s name and dates.
  • Guests may not use the laundry room alone.
  • Parking: guests use the visitor bays only.
    • Bays V1 to V4 are in the north lot.
    • Bay V5 is kept for deliveries.

Approval Steps

  1. Fill in the guest form.
  2. Send it to the membership committee.
  3. Wait for the written reply, usually within five working days.
Note: Guests who stay longer than 30 nights in a year count as occupants under the lease.

# Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I paint my walls?

Yes. Use a light color, and return the walls to white when you move out.

### Who fixes a broken appliance?

The cooperative fixes appliances that came with the home. Report the fault through the maintenance tracker.

### Can I sublet my home?

No. Subletting is not allowed under the lease.
